OUR STORY

More than
just stone.

Established in 2019, Denkyembo Marble & Granite has grown from a small fabrication outfit into a trusted name in Ghana's stone industry.

We work with homeowners, builders, designers and developers to bring carefully selected stone materials and quality workmanship together in one place.

Our approach is simple: understand what our clients need, use the right materials and pay attention to every detail from selection through installation.

02 / FABRICATION

Our fabrication team transforms raw marble, granite and engineered stone slabs into refined surfaces designed for each project.

From kitchen worktops and bathroom vanities to staircases, tables and custom pieces, every cut and finish is handled with care.
